Add Send to Trips to your browser

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Send to Trips

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + B in Chrome and Edge, View → Show Favourites Bar in Safari, View → Toolbars → Bookmarks Toolbar in Firefox.
  2. Drag the button onto it.
  3. On a Trip.com, Klook, Viator, Booking.com or hotel page, click Send to Trips. A new tab opens here: pick the trip and day, and the form fills itself in.

On an iPhone or iPad: bookmark any page, , then edit the bookmark and paste the code over its address.

It reads the page in your own browser, where sites such as Trip.com show it normally, though they turn the planner's server away. The page comes straight here and is only read when you press the button; nothing is sent anywhere else{% if claude %} except to Claude, to read it{% endif %}.
